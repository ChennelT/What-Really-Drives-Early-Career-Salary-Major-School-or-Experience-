# Project Overview 
Choosing a college major and preparing for a career are decisions that are often tied to expectations about future earnings. However, it is not always clear which factors actually have the greatest influence on early career salary outcomes. While some believe that attending a well-known school leads to higher pay, others argue that major choice or prior experience plays a more significant role.  

This project examines how early-career salary is associated with differences in college major, school, and prior exoerience. The goal is to better understand which of these factors is most strongly linked to higher starting salaries and whether common assumptions about education and earnings hold true.  

In doing so, the project examines:
* How does early career salary vary across different college majors?
* Do school characteristics meaningfully influence salary outcomes?
* Does prior experience, such as internships, lead to higher starting salaries?
* Which of these factors appears to have the strongest overall impact?
